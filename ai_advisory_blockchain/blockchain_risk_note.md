## stablecoin type and risks
There are 4 types of stable coins
1) Fiat Collaterized: Backed by Fiat reserves like USD
2) Algorithmic: Supply adjusting algorithm, no collateral
3) Commodity backed: Pegged to a physical commodity like gold
4) Crypto collaterized: backed by ivercolaterized crypto

Fiat collaterized stable coins are available in the market today and are used by few in the real world transactions.
Algorithmic stable coins are experimental and highly volatile at this stage.

## DeFi/DAO governance risk 
DAO are decentralized autonomous organizations. Token holders vote on protocol upgrades and other rules. They are an alternative to the centralized management, more transparent. But hey also face coordination, legal and security challenges

# crypto-as-an-asset-class recommendation for Paytm Money
Crypto currency are highly volatile assets. As per CAPM theory crypto do have any intrinsic value and hence cannot be included in a portfolio. As per Gordon Growth method if it is possible to evaluate future utiliy/value then we can consider it as a part of portfolio.

Data Driven approach:
Strongest defense for including Crypto in a portfolio comes from diversification perspective. Cryptos have been found to have very low or slighly negative corelation with equity, Dollar rates, Interest rates. It has a very low +ve correlation wiht Gold/Precious metals. This makes it a possible candidate to include in a portfolio as it does provide diversification. 

Another support for Crypto to be included in a portfolio comes from analysis of Alpha that crypto does generate. Sharpe ratio of Crypto is comparable to S&P. However what we are ignoring here is the fact that we are only studying returns of cryptos that survived and not considering thousands that failed, thus providing survivorship bias.

Goldman Sachs had moved 2.5% of their portfolio to Crypto and observed a 1.65% increase in returns. Hence based on this it can be considered as a very small part of portfolio.

Howevever from regulatory perspective in India, profits are taxed at 33% and there is a 1% transaction cost. Given these it is best to avoid crypto in portfolio for now and wait for costs to reduce and more clarity to come.

# T.A.N.G. (Temptation/Authority/Need/Greed) fraud framework 
## Two social-engineering risk vectors most relevant to a UPI/wallet + lending + wealth platform specifically
1) Urgent KYC submission: Scammers reach out to customers and use the Authority approach to get the customer share credentials like password/OTP etc. that can be used by the scamsters
2) Vishing: Scammers spoof calls to customers to get OTPs/ PINs / process loan applications etc. Scammers are using the Temptation and Greed part of the TANG framework to exploit customers

## One bank-side real-time defense mechanism that mitigates them
1) For KYC submission frauds, banks can block synthetic and deep fraud submissions, use passive liveness detection. Banks can also enforce in app only submission of KYC and educate customers about the same
2) Against Vising attacks, banks can build in app call verification and show in app if it is a genuine call. Banks can also use real time telemetry and behavior fraud detction to help customers
